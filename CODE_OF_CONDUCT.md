# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

TeachSpark is an open, welcoming project. Everyone who participates — through
issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other channel — is expected to
treat others with respect and professionalism.

## Standards

**We encourage:**
- Constructive, evidence-based feedback
- Clear and kind communication
- Welcoming contributors of all experience levels
- Focusing criticism on ideas, not people

**We do not tolerate:**
- Personal attacks or insults
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Sustained disruptive behaviour after being asked to stop
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate
  in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces (GitHub Issues, Pull
Requests, Discussions, and any other official channels) and to public
spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainer
at **support@sharesmallbiz.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and
treated with confidentiality.

Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that
do not align with this code of conduct, and to temporarily or permanently
ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful to the community.
